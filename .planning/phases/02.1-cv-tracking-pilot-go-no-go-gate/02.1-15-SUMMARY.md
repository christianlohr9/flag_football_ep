# Phase 02.1 Plan 15: Ground-Truth Position-Error Measurement Summary

**250 hand-labelled foot positions measured against the v1 tracking pipeline via `cv/accuracy.py`'s new GT contract: median 0.169 yd (0.154 m) / p90 0.415 yd (0.380 m) / max 1.527 yd (1.396 m) against the ~1.094 yd (1 m) C-09 threshold, 98.4% match rate, with a per-zone breakdown, an 8-pair known-distance sanity check, and an explicit error decomposition against the homography's own 0.27 yd reprojection error -- recorded as v1-pipeline pending a re-measurement after the parallel tracking rework.**

### Scope Notes -- Honest Findings, Not Auto-Fixed

**1. East-endzone has zero GT candidates in this session's tracked data**
- **Found during:** Task 2's automation (`ffep cv accuracy --prepare`)
- **What happened:** Direct inspection of the tracks parquet confirms no row (out of 341,461, either hover position) ever projects past `x_yards = 44.2` on a 50-yard field -- the real east end zone (`x >= 50`) was never reached by tracked action in this footage.
- **Handling:** Not worked around or fabricated. `prepare_gt_frames`'s frame-selection code is confirmed correct (verified via direct data inspection, not a selection-algorithm bug); the plan's own checkpoint resume-signal text ("...or describe what could not be marked") anticipates exactly this outcome. Documented in `docs/pilot-accuracy.md`'s `## Ground-Truth-Set` and `## Grenzen` as a Phase-2.0 capture-setup finding.
- **Verification:** `data/reference/video_inventory.csv`/tracks-parquet `x_yards` distribution inspected directly per hover position (hp-01 max 31.7 yd, hp-02 max 44.2 yd).

**2. Clip 8 contributed 7 statistically-correlated consecutive frames**
- **Found during:** Task 2 close-out, reviewing the filled GT CSV
- **What happened:** The user's labelling session marked 7 near-identical, temporally-adjacent frames (frame_index 1-7) from clip 8 -- not independent scenes.
- **Handling:** Reported honestly in `docs/pilot-accuracy.md`'s `## Ground-Truth-Set` as an "~15 effective independent scenes" caveat rather than presenting the nominal 21-frame/250-point count as if it were 21 independent samples. The measurement itself (`measure_position_error`) is unaffected -- it operates per matched point, not per scene -- but the *interpretation* of statistical power is adjusted.
- **Verification:** Direct groupby of `(clip_number, frame_index)` in the filled CSV.
