## Key findings from the sighting

1. **Own games are far richer than scouting films** (73–95 % vs 9–17 % fill rate) — quantified per file in the spec, confirming the user's observation.
2. **DEF FRONT availability is asymmetric and preset-dependent:** absent entirely from the 43-column own-game preset (GERvsSLO), but present and 94 % filled in the 45-column own-game preset (MEXvsGER) and present in the 12-column scouting preset. Note: this refines the checkpoint hypothesis ("rich preset has no DEF FRONT") — it holds for one of the two own-game presets, not both.
3. **BLITZ charts persons, not tactics:** values are exclusively player names (first names in some films, surnames in others). Per PII policy, no names entered the committed artifacts; the pattern is documented generically and queued for the analyst.
4. **Preset instability:** none of the three samples matches the 19-column contract baseline; spec now states the user must create/use the contract preset for forward exports.
5. **COVERAGE mixes three name spaces** (color calls, numeric shells like `3-1`/`F 1-2-1`, textbook `QUARTERS`); only three spelling variants needed normalization.
