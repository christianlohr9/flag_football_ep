<objective>
Bring the measurements from plans M2-02-01 and M2-02-02 into the documents the hackathon teams and
BWI actually read (BASE-02), give every method a start command that runs (BASE-03), record
Deep-EIoU's license wall as a result rather than a gap, and put the one decision this phase cannot
make — whether the submitted 90-percent target mark still holds — in front of the user with the
comparison in writing (BASE-04).

The measurement's central methodological finding must survive into the documents, because it changes
how the numbers may be read: `score_tracks.py` prints an automatic continuity rate that reacts to the
submitted tracks (BoT-SORT: 57/61 = 93,44 %) and a human reference rate that is read out of
`continuity_review.csv` and is the same for every submission (15/61 = 24,59 %). The human 24,59 %
belongs to BoT-SORT alone, because the human judgements were made on BoT-SORT overlays. Any table
that puts another method's number in that column would be fabricated, and any team that reads such a
table would optimise against a phantom.

Purpose: the challenge ships with honest numbers, and no team beats a straw man.
Output: `docs/baseline-messung.md`, a minimal, correct diff in both challenge-description documents,
a drift-proof test, and a user decision on the target mark.
</objective>
