<objective>
Measure the fourth method of BASE-01 that is measurable at all: Global Tracklet Association
(`gta-link`, MIT). GTA is not a tracker — it is post-processing that splits tracklets containing
more than one identity and merges tracklets belonging to the same identity, using appearance
embeddings plus DBSCAN. Its input here is the existing BoT-SORT tracks Parquet plus the 17,059
crops that already ship in the bundle; its output is a refined tracks file scored by the same
unmodified `score_tracks.py` as every other method.

Two license facts from RESEARCH decide the shape of this plan and are not up for re-litigation
during execution:
- `sjc042/gta-link` is MIT and `KaiyangZhou/deep-person-reid` is MIT — both are vendored at a pinned
  commit SHA, licenses re-verified at execution time.
- The PyPI package literally named `torchreid` is a THIRD-PARTY repackaging by a different
  maintainer and is `[ASSUMED]` — it must not be installed. The `sports_model.pth.tar-60` checkpoint
  that both `gta-link` and the unlicensed Deep-EIoU repo point at has no traceable license and must
  not be fetched. The generic, officially hosted OSNet checkpoint is used instead, and the
  substitution is reported as a limitation, not hidden.

Purpose: GTA is the one ready-made method that plausibly attacks the dominant failure mode
(39/46 fails are ID switches at player overlap), so BASE-04 needs its number.
Output: a pinned vendor record, one adapter script, its tests, and the GTA rows in
`data/reference/baseline-methods/`.
</objective>
