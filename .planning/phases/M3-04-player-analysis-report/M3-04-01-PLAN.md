<objective>
M3-04's research read the head coach's `Player Analysis All Camps` tab formula cells directly and
found two definitions in the finished M3-3 module that do not match what his workbook computes:

1. `Attempts` (cell `D2`) is `= B2 + C2 + H2` — Comps + Incs + INTs. **Sacks are a separate column
   and are never added in.** `features/explosiveness.py` currently states and implements the
   opposite (`HC_PASS_ATTEMPT_FILTER`: "Comps+Incs+Sacks", implemented as canonical
   `play_type == "pass"`, which includes sack rows). Every rate whose denominator is "his Attempts"
   is therefore computed on a denominator he never used.
2. `Efficiency` (cell `U2`) divides by `D2 + W2` — Attempts + **Carries** (both same-sheet cells).
   `hc_efficiency_table` divides by Attempts + Drops.

Fixing this is authorised cross-phase work: M3-3's implementation plans are executed, only its
human-review checkpoint (M3-03-03) is still open, and M3-4's whole product is "his numbers,
reproduced with his definitions" — shipping a report on a denominator he does not use would put
the error in front of him instead of removing it.

Purpose: every downstream plan in this phase consumes these functions. The correction has to land
before the report is built on top of them, not after.
Output: corrected module, regenerated committed CSVs, corrected German documents with a dated,
visible correction note, and tests that fail on the old denominators.
</objective>
