Dtype decision, and its reason: `drop` is `pl.Utf8`, not an integer flag. His own formula tests it
with the Excel wildcard criterion `"*"` (`COUNTIFS(..., Data!W, "*")`), which in Excel matches
**text only** — a numeric `1` in that cell would not be counted by his own sheet. Storing the raw
charted text preserves that distinction instead of erasing it into a boolean, and the report's flag
(`drop` non-null and non-empty after stripping) is deliberately more permissive than his formula.
That divergence is exactly what Frage 9 asks about (M3-04-07); record it, do not resolve it.
