<objective>
Prove, component by component, what license every delivered piece of the challenge carries
(RECHT-04): the `ffep` package and the scoring script the teams run, every distribution the teams
install to use them, the model artefacts behind the shipped detections, and the data artefacts in
the three bundles. The reconciliation in `ABGLEICH.md` states the own chain is AGPL-free
(rfdetr Apache-2.0, trackers Apache-2.0, supervision MIT) — this plan turns that statement into a
verified table with a named source per row, and keeps it from rotting via a test.

Purpose: the federation must be able to adopt a hackathon result; a license surprise in the
delivered chain would make that impossible, and the challenge description already promises the
teams a permissive base.
Output: `docs/lizenz-inventur.md`, `tests/test_m2_lizenz_inventur.py`, and one pending todo for
the one decision this inventory cannot make on its own.
</objective>
