<objective>
Produce the two bundle inputs the hackathon dev-set needs and 2.1 never persisted: a per-frame
detections Parquet (so teams never have to run a detector) and the torso crops (training material
for appearance models), both pinned to the frozen detector run.

Purpose: the challenge promises "Detektionen pro Frame als Parquet" and "~17.000 Spielerinnen-Crops"
(docs/hackathon-challenge-reid.md §Verfügbare Daten). Those artifacts must exist, carry their
provenance, and be reproducible from the pin.
Output: two implemented export functions with tests, and the exported artifacts for the pilot
session under the gitignored label tree.
</objective>
