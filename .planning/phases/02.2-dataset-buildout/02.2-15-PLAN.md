<objective>
Train the detector on the iteration-1 multi-domain dataset and measure what it bought — per domain,
on the frozen eval clips, against the pre-committed threshold from docs/dataset-plan.md.

Purpose: D-14 sets no hard gate, which makes the reporting discipline the whole point. A pooled mAP
number would hide exactly the thing this phase is trying to establish: whether a second domain
helps or dilutes.
Output: an implemented `evaluate_per_domain`, a registered detector run, a per-domain comparison
table, and the stopping-rule verdict for iteration 2.
</objective>
