<objective>
Put the two never-piloted domains through the existing sighting pipeline: parameterise
`cv/sighting.py` and `cv/frames.py` by domain (both currently hard-filter `domain == "drone"`),
run the sighting against the 60 GoPro sideline clips and the 51 TV broadcast clips, refresh the
stale inventory notes, and hand the findings to the user for the D-11 decision.

Purpose: the domain mix (D-10) cannot be proposed on assumption. Everything downstream — mix,
eval splits, per-domain inference settings — depends on measured apparent player size and tier.
Output: two per-session sighting CSVs, a German sighting record, refreshed inventory notes, and a
user verdict on whether the sideline material is usable for detection training.
</objective>
