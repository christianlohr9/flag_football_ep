<objective>
Close the hackathon's most important labelling gap: the 41 unreviewed pilot clips (A) and the
flag-pull bonus-metric events (B). Both are user handwork of roughly one hour each, and both are
prerequisites for a dev/test-set split that does not leak.

Purpose: without the remaining 41 verdicts, the 20 already-reviewed clips become the de-facto test
set and every hackathon team optimises against them. This is the single highest-value hour in the
whole preparation (docs/hackathon-challenge-prep.md §2, "Wichtigste Stunde").
Output: a complete 61-row continuity review, a filled flag-pull event table, a recomputed baseline
number with its denominator, and a schema gate over both tables.
</objective>
