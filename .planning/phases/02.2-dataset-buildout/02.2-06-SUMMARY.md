## Decisions Made

- **Metadata-only inventory read instead of `clip_paths`:** `freeze_eval_clips` reads clip numbers and session ids straight from `video_inventory.csv` (mirroring `_read_clip_durations`'s existing pattern) rather than calling `clip_paths`, which enforces that the actual video file exists on disk. This is the correct design on its own terms (a stratification/bookkeeping function has no reason to require video bytes), and it was also necessary in this session since `data/video/*` is gitignored and not present in this parallel worktree.
- **Second eval-frozen domain is GoPro/sideline, not TV/broadcast:** the plan's Task 1 literally names `drone` and "the second training domain" (singular). Given D-11's ratification admits GoPro unconditionally and TV only pending its training-time mAP ablation, GoPro is the domain that is unambiguously "in scope" for a frozen eval split right now. TV's own frozen eval split is deferred to a later plan if/when the ablation confirms TV as a first-class domain needing an independent per-domain mAP report — documented explicitly in `docs/dataset-plan.md` section 8 as a forward pointer, not a silent omission.
- **Seed-set verdict `nicht übernommen`, honestly reasoned:** the required programmatic per-range spot-check could not run in this isolated worktree (see Deviations below). Rather than assert an unverified "übernommen" or invent numbers, the conservative verdict was recorded with the reason spelled out. The mix section already excludes the pilot's 304 frames from the drone frame target, so this verdict required no numeric restatement — it was designed consistently from the start.
- **Domain mix expanded to three domains:** `docs/dataset-plan.md`'s mix (drone 900 / GoPro-sideline 400 / TV-broadcast 200) reflects `docs/material-sighting.md`'s actual Ratifizierungs-Block (both GoPro and TV admitted as training domains, TV conditional) rather than CONTEXT.md's earlier "drone + one second domain" framing, which predates the D-11 ratification.
