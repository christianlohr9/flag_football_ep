## Decisions Made

- **D-11 ratified (conditional, dual-domain):** the user's verbatim verdict — "Grundsätzlich 'mehr ist immer besser' — beide Domänen nehmen. Leidet darunter die Qualität der Modelle, dann nur GoPro. Sollten wir uns damit schlechter stellen, bitte nur GoPro. Sonst beides." — is recorded verbatim in `docs/material-sighting.md`'s `## Ratifizierungs-Block`. Operationalised as: both domains admitted now; training runs (02.2-15/02.2-18) must run a per-domain mAP ablation (with vs. without TV) on the frozen per-domain eval splits (D-04/D-13); if TV measurably degrades drone or GoPro per-domain mAP, TV drops back to transfer-only. This makes the user's condition measurable and keeps the worst case identical to GoPro-only, backed by evidence.
- **Geometry correction:** GoPro = Hinterfeld-/Endzone-Ansicht von hinten (not a side view despite the `sideline` domain label); TV = the actual Seitenansicht and the geometric match for capture-protocol's Domäne 2. This is a documentation-only correction — `domain` column values and the `--domain` CLI vocabulary are unchanged, since `02.2-06` and the sighting pipeline key on them.
- **Scope boundary held:** TV's admission as a training domain is scoped to detection only. Tracking on moving TV cameras remains deferred to Phase 2.5 per REQ-S2-06 — this ratification does not touch that deferral.
