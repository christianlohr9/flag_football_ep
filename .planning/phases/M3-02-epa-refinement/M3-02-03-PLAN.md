<objective>
Lift the head coach's own method out of Excel and into the repository as plain, quotable data. His
`Scoring Probability by Situation 2023-2026.xlsx` holds nine aggregate tabs — `SP by D&D`,
`EP by D&D`, `Sample Size by D&D`, the three `Clustered` variants, the two `weighted` variants, and
`Reg` — and M3-02-RESEARCH section 4 verified this session that all nine contain only down,
distance, field-half, probability, count and regression-coefficient values: no player name, no team
name, no real date. They are safe to snapshot verbatim (EPA-D03).

Two things make this more than a copy-paste:

- **`Sample Size by D&D` is the point of the whole comparison.** Several own-half cells at distance
  >= 10 carry n in the low teens, next to opposite-half cells with n in the hundreds for the same
  (down, distance). Without n beside every probability, his numbers and the model's look equally
  confident, and the honest argument M3-02-07 has to make disappears.
- **Three of his clustered bin labels are corrupted by Excel.** `"1-5"`, `"6-10"` and `"11-15"` were
  silently autocorrected into `datetime(2021, 1, 5)`, `datetime(2021, 6, 10)` and
  `datetime(2021, 11, 15)` — a well-known Excel trap, not a data-entry error by him. `openpyxl`
  returns genuine `datetime` objects with nothing signalling the corruption (RESEARCH Pitfall 4).
  They must be written as reconstructed text labels, marked `[ASSUMED]`, with the reasoning attached.

This plan is independent of the corpus fixes (M3-02-01/02) and of the training run — it reads a
static workbook and writes static CSVs, so it runs in wave 1 alongside them.

Purpose: M3-02-06 needs his numbers in a joinable shape to put ours next to them; M3-02-07 needs
them quotable and reproducible for the October sync.
Output: `scripts/hc_sp_snapshot.py`, ten CSVs under `data/reference/hc_sp_tables/`,
`tests/test_m3_epa_snapshot.py`.
</objective>

Sheet names present in `Scoring Probability by Situation 2023-2026.xlsx`:
`Data`, `Copy of Data`, `SP by D&D`, `EP by D&D`, `Sample Size by D&D`, `SP by D&D Clustered`,
`EP by D&D Clustered`, `General Stats`, `Sample Size by D&D Clustered`, `SP by D&D weighted`,
`EP by D&D weighted`, `Reg`, `1st down OppH EPA`, `2nd down OppH EPA`, `3rd down OppH EPA`,
`D&D Frequency weighted`, `GainLoss Frequency`, `GainLoss Percentage`.

Axis structure of `SP by D&D` / `EP by D&D` / `Sample Size by D&D`: rows are distance-to-go
(1..14+, whole yards), columns are down (1..4) crossed with field half (`Own Half` /
`Opposite Half` — which side of midfield the play started on). Spot values confirmed this session:
down=1 / distance=1 / own half has SP 0.667 on n=21; down=1 / distance=10 / own half has n=324.

`Clustered` variants use distance bins. Their surviving, uncorrupted row labels are `16-19` /
`16-20` (own-half and opposite-half columns use slightly different edges), `20.0` / `21-25`, and
`20+` / `25+`. The first three labels come back as
`datetime.datetime(2021, 1, 5)`, `datetime.datetime(2021, 6, 10)`,
`datetime.datetime(2021, 11, 15)` and reconstruct to `1-5`, `6-10`, `11-15` (`[ASSUMED]`, RESEARCH
section 4.3 and assumption A2).

`Reg` tab columns: `Half, Down, Distance, Code, Emp, Forecast, Diff, Reg, Diff, Chosen Ones`. Column
`F` holds live `=FORECAST(x, known_ys, known_xs)` calls against fixed `Copy of Data` row ranges,
multiplied by 6. Column `H` holds hardcoded polynomial formulas with literal coefficients whose
degree rises per down and field half, e.g. `-0.0595*Distance + 4.23` (down 1, own half) up to a
degree-3-and-higher form for down 4. Read these with `data_only=False` to get the formula strings.
</interfaces>
