<objective>
The last gate before the October sync. Everything measured in this phase is now on paper; what a
test cannot decide is whether it is the right thing to hand a colleague, and whether the retrained
models should become the production champion.

Three judgements belong to the user and to nobody else:

1. **Tone and framing.** The document says, with numbers, that several cells of the head coach's own
   method rest on a dozen plays, that his `Reg` tab looks like hand-transcribed chart trendlines, and
   that two of his three workbooks are currently unusable. Every one of those is defensible and
   evidence-backed. Whether they are phrased in a way he will hear as help rather than critique is a
   relationship judgement no assertion can make.
2. **The champion promotion.** M3-02-05 registered new EP and WP versions and deliberately moved no
   alias — `docs/model-training.md` section 3 makes promotion an explicit, reviewed step. Whether
   the enlarged-corpus models become the champion is a decision, and if EP still does not beat its
   naive baseline it is a decision with a real argument on both sides.
3. **Which questions actually go out.** M3-2 adds two Zusatzfragen; M3-3 adds three; Fragen 1-3 are
   still unanswered from the September round. Whether all of them go to the head coach at once, and
   in what order of priority, is the user's call.

Purpose: this is the point where a repository document becomes something that leaves the repository.
Output: a reviewed `docs/epa-refinement-2026-10.md` with its status and the promotion decision
recorded, and any wording changes the review produced.
</objective>
