<objective>
Fix the two ingest-side defects that keep 100 % of the head coach's rows out of `plays.parquet`
(todo `2026-09-03-m3-2-eingangsbefunde-hc-korpus.md`, both reproduced against the real workbooks in
M3-02-RESEARCH §1 and §2).

1. `_split_pair_block` starts a new "game" whenever the **ordered** `(team1, team2)` pair changes.
   A real international game charted with alternating possession (`Germany | Ireland` →
   `Ireland | Germany` → `Germany | Ireland` …) therefore fragments into dozens of 4-10-row
   "games". On the real `Scoring Probability` `Data` tab's pair block this turns 22 contiguous games
   into 137 fragments. Comparing the **unordered** pair (`frozenset`) instead fixes it.
2. Every head-coach row gets `half = null`, which fails the `half_assigned` check for every one of
   the 2,128 games. Research §2.2 pins down why `null` and `1` are both wrong and `2` is right:
   `_mark_half_end` only fires `game_end` on `half == 2`, and `game_end` is what resolves WP's
   `Winner` and EP's post-game `None`-ing. `2` is the only value that satisfies `half_assigned`'s
   real contract (`half ∈ {1,2}`) instead of bypassing it.

The discipline that makes this a fix and not a wave-through: the sentinel applies **only** to games
a maintainer has actually declared in `hc_games.csv`, and **never** to the `Copy of Data` tab, whose
column layout is a different, unresolved problem (Frage 2, sharpened by RESEARCH §1.3's
header-mismatch finding). Everything undeclared keeps `half = null` and keeps quarantining.

Purpose: without both fixes, M3-02-04's ingest run produces zero trainable head-coach rows and the
whole phase has nothing to retrain on.
Output: `src/flag_football_ep/ingest/hc_workbook.py`, `tests/test_ingest_hc_workbook.py`,
`docs/data-contract.md`.
</objective>
