    Behaviour, in order: call `estimate_pat_baselines(plays)` and
    `estimate_fourth_down_rates(plays)` — reuse, never reimplement either estimator. Render both
    Figures via `render_pat_breakeven` and `render_fourth_down`, embed each through
    `reports.render.fig_to_data_uri` (never `fig.savefig`, so the close-in-`finally` discipline
    stays in one place), then call `render_page("decisions_cheatsheet.html.j2", ...)` and return
    the resulting string. The function performs no filesystem writes — plan 12's `build.py` owns
    every write.

    Derive these plain-German readings and pass them to the template as an ordered list of
    short sentences, so the page is usable even by someone who does not read charts:
    - the break-even 2-point rate as `baselines.one_point_rate / 2`, phrased as
      `Ab einer 2-Punkte-Rate von {rate:.0%} lohnt sich der 2-Punkte-Versuch.`
    - the observed 2-point rate with its attempt count and interval, phrased as
      `Beobachtet: {two_point_rate:.0%} (n={two_point_attempts}, CI {low:.0%}–{high:.0%}).`
    - the resulting verdict — whether the observed 2-point rate lies above, below, or with its
      interval straddling the break-even rate — as one of three explicit sentences. Straddling
      must produce the honest `Zu dünne Datenlage für eine klare Empfehlung.` reading, not a
      false-confidence go/no-go.
    - the 4th-down reading: the best and worst distance bucket by conversion rate, each with its
      n, and the overall 4th-down conversion rate with its n. Buckets with zero attempts are
      named as `keine Daten`, never as 0%.
    All numbers appear with their sample size in these sentences — the project's locked
    statistical-honesty bias applies to prose as much as to tables.

    A corpus with zero PAT attempts makes `estimate_pat_baselines` raise
    `InsufficientPatAttempts`; catch it in `build_decisions_page`, render the page with the
    4th-down half only, and pass an explicit German `Hinweis` string for the PAT half rather
    than letting a whole report run die on a thin corpus. State that behaviour in the docstring.
