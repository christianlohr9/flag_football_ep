## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-1.4-13 | Tampering (data integrity) | dropping a scoring play from the annotations in favour of a bigger pure swing | mitigate | `select_wp_annotations` ranks scoring/turnover rows ahead of swing-only rows before applying `max_markers`; asserted in tests |
