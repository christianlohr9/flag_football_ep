# Phase 01.4 Plan 08: Opponent Tendency Report Data Summary

**`flag_football_ep.reports.opponent` computes all four REQ-S1-12 tendency content areas for one opponent as a typed `OpponentReportData` object -- formation/route (Hudl-only, degrading gracefully), play-call and 4th-down/PAT behaviour (all sources, always-padded buckets), and a pooled-baseline plain-German summary -- ready for plan 10 to render with no arithmetic of its own.**

## Accomplishments

- `formation_tendencies` and `route_distribution` restrict to Hudl-charted rows via `charted_only`, build `share_table`s keyed on formation/down/distance/zone and target-route/down-distance/zone respectively, and take their `SectionBasis` from the restricted subset -- a fully-uncharted corpus (the sportapp/IFAF case) yields a zero-height table, a non-`None` `empty_notice`, and `basis.n_plays == 0` with no exception
- `route_distribution` folds two `share_table` groupings (down & distance, field zone) into one long-format table via an explicit `gruppierung`/`gruppe` schema, so a template renders two sub-tables from one frame
- `playcall_by_score_state` and `fourth_down_and_pat_behavior` pull from the full opponent frame (all sources), reusing `estimate_fourth_down_rates`'s attempt/conversion predicates and `estimate_pat_baselines`'s PAT-type predicates verbatim so the report and the decision charts never define "4th-down conversion" or "PAT type" differently
- `fourth_down_and_pat_behavior` always emits the full declared bucket set (4 distance buckets x go-rate and conversion-rate, plus PAT choice and PAT success by type) even with zero attempts -- `n=0`, `rate=null` rows rather than silently missing rows, mirroring `FourthDownRates`'s always-full-buckets contract
- `top_tendency_sentences` scores candidate cells against a pooled per-section baseline weighted by `sqrt(n)`, considers only cells with `n >= MUTED_MIN_N`, caps at two sentences per section, and falls back to a single "zu dünn" sentence naming the total play count when nothing clears the threshold -- every returned sentence carries an `n=` substring
- `build_opponent_data` assembles all four sections plus `top_tendency_sentences` into one `OpponentReportData`, collects German notices for degraded conditions (no charted games, fewer than two games, any empty section), and short-circuits a team absent from the corpus to empty sections, a single "keine Daten" summary sentence, and a notice -- never raising
