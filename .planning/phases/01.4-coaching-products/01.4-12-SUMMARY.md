# Phase 01.4 Plan 12: Own-Team Report Rendering Summary

**`build_own_team_page` renders plan 09's `OwnTeamReportData` into one standalone German HTML file with three embedded EPA/share charts, a numeric EPA-provenance footnote (out-of-fold vs champion play counts), and the unmapped-player warning above every table.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~45 min (single agent session)
- **Completed:** 2026-08-21
- **Tasks:** 2/2 completed (committed together, see Deviations)
- **Files modified:** 3 (1 modified, 2 created)

## Accomplishments

- `build_own_team_page(data, *, generated_on=None) -> str` performs no filesystem writes and turns an `OwnTeamReportData` into one self-contained `<!DOCTYPE html>` string, with every rendered `Figure` closed via `fig_to_data_uri` (`matplotlib.pyplot.get_fignums()` empty after every call, verified directly).
- Every numeric cell reaching the template is pre-formatted before render: EPA cells carry a leading sign, two decimals and `(n=K)` or render `keine Daten`; percentages carry their count; a stat that does not apply to a row (a QB's YAC columns) renders an en dash, distinct from "no data".
- Three charts embed via `fig_to_data_uri`: `render_epa_bars` over `efficiency_by_call`'s `off_play` rows only (with a caption pointing to the table below for formation/route), `render_epa_bars` over `player_efficiency`'s receiver rows only, and `render_share_bars` over `drive_outcomes`' outcome shares. A section whose table is empty contributes no chart, only its `empty_notice`.
- `own_team_report.html.j2` extends `base.html.j2`: a `.summary-block` headline (points per drive, cycle EPA/play), the unmapped-player `.warning-block` positioned above the first `<h2>` whenever `unmapped_players` is non-empty, a second `.warning-block` for `notices`, one section per `ReportSection` with its chart/table, and footnotes stating the EPA-provenance sentence, the current-cycle definition, and the Clopper-Pearson/muting-threshold methodology.
- `OwnTeamReportData` gained the raw scalars (`n_epa_oof`/`n_epa_champion`/`n_epa_none`, `overall_epa_play_cycle`/`overall_epa_play_n_cycle`) plan 12 needed but plan 09's version did not expose -- computed once in `build_own_team_data` over the union of the team's offense and defense plays (never double-counted, since a play's `posteam` and `defteam` are never equal).

## Decisions Made

- Extended `OwnTeamReportData` (plan 09's dataclass, same file) with `n_epa_oof`/`n_epa_champion`/`n_epa_none` and `overall_epa_play_cycle`/`overall_epa_play_n_cycle` rather than re-deriving them from the already-rolled-up `sections` tables at render time -- the raw `epa_source` distribution and a whole-cycle EPA mean are not recoverable from any of the four sections' per-dimension/per-player rollups, so the data has to be computed once, in `build_own_team_data`, where the row-level `epa`/`epa_source` columns are still in scope.
- The EPA-provenance counts are scoped to the team's offense + defense plays combined (every play the report actually covers), not the whole corpus -- a play can never be both, so the union never double-counts, and this is a tighter, more directly relevant scope than "every play in the dataset" would be.
- Chose one chart per section maximum (three total, defense none) rather than one chart per dimension, per the plan's explicit instruction that "one chart per dimension is too many for a tablet page" -- formation and route breakdowns, and QB rows, stay table-only with a caption pointing there.
- `_NOT_APPLICABLE` ("–", en dash) is a deliberately distinct display value from `_NO_DATA` ("keine Daten") -- a QB row's YAC columns are not missing data, they are a stat that role does not have; conflating the two would misrepresent an intentional gap as a data quality problem, which runs against this project's statistical-honesty bias.
