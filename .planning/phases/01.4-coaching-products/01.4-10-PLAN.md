<objective>
Provide the two generic, Figure-returning bar renderers the opponent tendency report and the
own-team efficiency report both embed: a share-distribution chart and a signed EPA-per-play
chart.

Purpose: CONTEXT's density decision is "one-pager-first ... full tables/charts follow below",
and its chart decision is static matplotlib images sized to screen width. Both report families
need the same two chart shapes over the same aggregation-table column contract, so building
them once here keeps plans 11 and 12 free of plotting code and keeps the muted/thin-sample
convention identical across every product.
Output: `charts/tendency.py`, following the established `charts/` package convention.
</objective>
