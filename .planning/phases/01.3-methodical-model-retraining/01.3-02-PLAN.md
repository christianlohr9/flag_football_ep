<objective>
Build the real canonical training corpus, profile the two columns the feature-candidate
experiments depend on (`game_date`, `competition`), and land the competition-tier reference
data plus its loader.

Purpose: RESEARCH Assumptions A2 and A3 record that `game_date`'s per-source null rate and

additionally requires the tier lookup to be keyed on `(source, competition)` pairs, mirroring
`team_mapping.csv`, not on `competition` alone. This plan removes both unknowns before any
experiment is designed.

Output: `data/processed/plays.parquet`, `01.3-DATA-PROFILE.md`, `data/reference/competition_tier.csv`,
`load_competition_tier`/`map_competition_tier` in `reference.py`, and the config/TOML wiring.
