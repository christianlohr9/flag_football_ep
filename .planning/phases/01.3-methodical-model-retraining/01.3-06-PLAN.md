</interfaces>

**Why not a normal-approximation CI.** RESEARCH flags that PAT attempt counts in a
single-season flag-football corpus land in the tens-to-low-hundreds and PAT success rates are
often extreme (>90%) — exactly where `p ± 1.96*sqrt(p(1-p)/n)` breaks down. Use
`scipy.stats.binomtest(...).proportion_ci()`; do not hand-roll.

**Break-even arithmetic.** A 1-point try is worth `1 * p1` expected points; a 2-point try is
worth `2 * p2`. Going for 2 is the higher-EV call exactly when `p2 > p1 / 2`. That crossing
point is what the chart must make legible, with the dataset's own `p1` and `p2` (and their CIs)
marked on it.
