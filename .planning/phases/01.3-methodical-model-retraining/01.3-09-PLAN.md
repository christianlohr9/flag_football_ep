Decide the four feature-candidate adoptions together, retrain EP and WP on the full canonical
corpus under the final feature set, promote a reviewed champion for each model, and write the
consolidated training report plus operator documentation.

Purpose: this is the phase gate. Plans 01.3-04 through 01.3-08 built the honest evaluation
protocol, the calibration report, the empirical PAT baselines and the four candidate verdicts;
this plan turns them into a shipped, versioned, reviewed pair of models and the evidence trail
the ROADMAP's five success criteria are checked against. CONTEXT §Promotion requires the
champion alias to be set through an explicit `ffep promote` "used after reviewing the training
report" — so the review is a real checkpoint, not a formality.

Output: the adoption decision applied to `hyperparams.py`, retrained and promoted models,
`01.3-TRAINING-REPORT.md`, `docs/model-training.md`, and an updated `docs/pipeline.md`.

adopted, extend `EP_FEATURES`/`WP_FEATURES` and the `*_TRAINING_COLUMNS` supersets — but
