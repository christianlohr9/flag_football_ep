<objective>
Make the head coach's workbooks a source of `ffep ingest`, decide honestly which of his plays we
already have, and write down in German what actually happened.

Dedupe follows his own preference (HC-D03: "wir reichern eher unsere Daten um seine an … Duplikate
bei ihm erkennen und nicht berücksichtigen; sonst mit der Doppelung leben"): stage 1 pairs games
through the maintained `corpus_game_id` column, stage 2 excludes the head-coach rows whose content
fingerprint matches a row of the paired game, and everything that does not pair is kept and counted
rather than quietly deleted. Today the real overlap cases are the `Data` / `Copy of Data`
duplication inside the Scoring-Probability workbook and the WC-2026 games we already hold from IFAF
— the current `games.parquet` snapshot contains no `hudl`-source game at all, so an HC↔Hudl overlap
would be zero by definition, and the report must say that instead of implying we checked something
we could not.

Purpose: after this plan, M3-2 can train on one canonical corpus and the head coach can read, in
German, what we took from his files, what we skipped, and what we still need from him.
Output: `ingest/hc_dedupe.py`, the pipeline/CLI wiring, real `hc_games.csv` rows from a real run,
`docs/hc-workbook-ingest.md`, and a PII gate test over every committed artefact of this phase.
</objective>
