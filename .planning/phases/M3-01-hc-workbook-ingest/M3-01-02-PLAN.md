<objective>
Give the project the ability to read the head coach's workbooks at all, and to read them honestly:
`openpyxl` as a declared, licence-inventoried core dependency, plus the reading half of
`ingest/hc_workbook.py` — sheet reader, dtype-based block segmentation, and per-block mapping onto
the contract's raw column names.

The hard part is not the file format, it is that one sheet is not one table. `Scoring Probability
by Situation 2023-2026.xlsx`'s `Data` and `Copy of Data` tabs carry two incompatible row layouts
under a single header row, and `Germany Analytics Stats EC 2025 vs WC Nations.xlsx`'s `Data` tab
carries a correct header over 2,506 completely empty rows. Both must come out of this module as
named, counted findings.

Purpose: everything downstream (game identity, canonical derivation, dedupe) is straightforward
once the rows are correctly segmented and correctly labelled. A wrong block boundary here would
silently mis-attribute passer, receiver and gain for years of charting.
Output: `openpyxl` in `pyproject.toml`/`uv.lock`/`docs/lizenz-inventur.md`, two new config paths,
four new canonical extras, `src/flag_football_ep/ingest/hc_workbook.py` (reading half) and
`tests/test_ingest_hc_workbook.py` built entirely on synthetic workbooks.
</objective>
