1. **Contract v1.2.** His charting uses six RESULT values our 13-token vocabulary does not know
   (`Block`, `Dropped`, `Timeout`, `Batted Down`, `Offsetting Penalties`, `Blocked, Def TD`). The
   user approved adding them ("Jona ist HC, er schlägt alles", 2026-09-03), so they enter the fixed

the structurally clean third workbook.
