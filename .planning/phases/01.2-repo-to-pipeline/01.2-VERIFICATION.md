This was independently reproduced in this session (not merely cited from the review): sorting a payload containing one play with `playNumber: null` raises `TypeError`, and that exception is positioned to drop every IFAF game in the run. None of plans 18-24 touched this path -- plan 20 (the containment gap-closure plan) explicitly scoped its `files_modified` to `hudl.py`/`sportapp.py`/`checks.py`, and plan 23's edit to `ifaf.py` was for an unrelated fix (WR-03, defensive-conversion crediting).

This directly contradicts the phase's own "Per-file / per-source ingest failures are contained and visibly reported" truth -- established during the original verification and the basis for the entire CR-02 gap-closure effort -- for one of the phase's three required sources. Given REQ-S1-06 explicitly requires IFAF tournament data to be part of the merged canonical dataset, and the fresh review measured this against 32 real WM-2026 games currently in the live IFAF feed, this is classified as a blocking gap rather than a lower-severity warning.

**Not treated as phase gaps (carried forward from the initial verification, still true):** the unrotated sportapp.fi API key is a documented, user-deferred operator action recorded in STATE.md. Reference-data partial seeding and legacy placeholder team codes are documented user decisions. The lower-severity Warning/Info findings from the fresh review (WR-02 memory exhaustion risk, WR-03 Hudl penalty-play_type asymmetry, WR-04 IFAF play_type always null, WR-05 IFAF drive_id-starts-at-0, WR-06 sportapp operator-precedence latent risk, and the nine Info items) are real but do not, individually, threaten the three roadmap success criteria the way the IFAF containment gap does; they are listed under Anti-Patterns for visibility and should be tracked for a future plan.
