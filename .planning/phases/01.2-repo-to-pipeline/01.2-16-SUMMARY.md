## Decisions Made

- **EP legacy-subset boundary derived at runtime, not hardcoded:** `scripts/capture_notebook_baseline.py`'s `build_ep_frame` concatenates the legacy CSV's rows before the WC24 corpus's rows (`pl.concat([df, df_wc], how="diagonal")`), and neither `prepare_ep_data` nor `make_ep_model_mutations` reorders rows -- so the baseline's first `n_legacy` rows are exactly the legacy portion, where `n_legacy` is the package's own legacy-only chain's row count (verified: `yardline_50`/`yards_to_go`/`down0`..`down4` match the baseline subset exactly to the float, confirming the boundary is correct, not coincidental).
- **Three newly-discovered deltas documented rather than silently tolerated or left to fail the test** (full detail in `tests/test_migration_equivalence.py`'s module docstring):
  1. EP/WP `label` distribution differs because the frozen notebook's `scoring_play_team` derivation (`Python/helper_add_hudl_mutations.py`) chains `flag_a | flag_b | flag_c == 1` inside one `pl.when` -- Python/polars operator precedence binds `==` tighter than `|`, so only the *last* flag is ever compared to 1. `canonical.add_scoring_play_team` (already shipped, its own docstring documents this exact fix) corrects it; every downstream `Next_Score_Half`/`Winner` chain inherits the fix.
  2. WP's `receive_2h_ko` differs because the frozen notebook's `prepare_wp_data` redefines `start_posteam` a second time without the first definition's `.forward_fill()`, making it non-null only on each game's first play. `features.mutations.prepare_wp_data` (already shipped) keeps one `.forward_fill().over("game_id")`-scoped `start_posteam`.
  3. EP's `Total_W_Scaled` is corpus-dependent (pre-authorized by this plan's `<action>` block): the baseline's weights were computed over legacy+WC24 combined, this test's legacy-only chain computes them over legacy alone.
  All three are within CONTEXT.md's "obvious fixes only" porting policy (silent logic bugs, not methodology changes) and were already shipped by earlier plans -- this test's job was to detect and document them, not to fix or revert them.
- **Synthetic Hudl fixture redesign for `test_cli_run.py`:** the straightforward multi-source `full_tree` fixture from `test_pipeline_ingest.py` (14 rows) hit `DegenerateWeightRange` (zero variation in `Drive_Score_Dist`) because every drive in that fixture scores on its own last play. `test_cli_run.py`'s fixture instead alternates "turnover" drives (closed by a `Rush, Fumble`, no score) with "score" drives, and flips `ODK` on turnover drives so `posteam` (and the WP label) varies -- confirmed by direct experimentation before committing to the design.
