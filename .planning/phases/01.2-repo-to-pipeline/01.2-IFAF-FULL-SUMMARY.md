# Phase 01.2: IFAF Full Snapshot + Yardage/Yards-to-Go Derivation Summary

**Snapshotted all 96 cpx.studio games (both ffwc26-women and ffwc26-men) via two never-before-fetched endpoints with PII redaction, derived both yards_gained and yards_to_go (100% coverage, from IFAF's own four-downs-to-midfield/four-downs-to-score rule) for the canonical corpus, fixed a live-corpus data-quality regression per-game via a real-validation-suite-driven snapshot choice, and — after discovering the men's tournament had silently merged into the women's corpus identity via both a shared competition label and a colliding team code — made the corpus safe by construction: ingest itself is now scoped to sources.ifaf.ingest_tournaments (women only by default), with distinct team codes standing by for the opt-in case.**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 5 initial (fetch layer, yardage derivation, ingest/score re-run, video marks, docs) + 4 follow-up rounds (yards_to_go derivation, corpus-regression fix, competition-mislabelling fix, ingest-time tournament scoping)
- **Files modified/created:** 24 total across the whole session (16 source/config files modified across all rounds, 4 new source/test files, 4 docs created/updated)
- **Commits:** 15 production commits (see below) + this summary

## Accomplishments

- All 96 games (48 women + 48 men) snapshotted with `game_{id}.json`/`plays_{id}.json` added alongside the existing `unified-plays_{id}.json`/`events_{id}.json` — 389 files total, all PII-redacted at the fetch layer
- `yards_gained` derivation with explicit, tested priority rules (penalty > touchdown > safety > turnover-shaped > same-drive > null)
- `yards_to_go` derivation from IFAF's own four-downs-to-midfield/four-downs-to-goal rule — field position alone determines the line to gain; empirically validated against the events feed's own MIDDLE/GOAL marker (98.2% agreement for the simple per-play version chosen, vs. 74.3% for a "sticky" drive-persistent alternative that was tried and rejected on the evidence)
- `play_type` coverage raised via a sequence-derived fallback, using only already-contract-approved tokens
- **Real EP/WP model output unlocked for IFAF**: `ep`/`epa` non-null on ~98% of accepted rows (women 98.0%, men 97.8%), `wp` on 100%, `wpa` on ~99% — up from ~0% (structural, `yards_to_go` being a required EP_FEATURES column) before the `yards_to_go` derivation existed
- Discovered, quantified, and then **fixed** a live-corpus data-quality regression between the 2026-08-17 and 2026-09-06 snapshots (11 women's games had fewer plays and gained null `down` values) via a per-game "use whichever snapshot the real validation suite accepts" policy — women's-bracket acceptance restored to exactly 32/48, matching the original 2026-08-17 baseline
- Built a per-play video-mark table (5,522 plays, 62 games, 70% with a resolvable video URL); confirmed one sampled video is publicly reachable via HEAD request (no download)
- **Discovered and fixed a competition-mislabelling bug**: both tournament documents share the identical `tournament.name`, so all 25 accepted men's games had silently joined the women's competition label end-to-end. Fixed with a division-aware label, a new `mens-international` competition tier, exclusion of all 48 known men's game ids from EP/WP training (`ffep.toml`), and a corpus-scope filter in the M3 explosiveness/comparison script — which was especially exposed since `m-ger`/`w-ger` collide onto the same canonical team code `GER`
- **Went further after a fourth follow-up: made the corpus safe by construction, not by downstream exclusion.** Training-only exclusion still left men's rows in `plays.parquet` under the colliding `GER` team code, leaking into every team-scoped report/HC-split/`scrimmage_plays` caller with no exclusion list of its own. Fixed by gating `ingest_snapshots` itself on `sources.ifaf.ingest_tournaments` (defaults to women only; the men's tournament is still snapshotted but never becomes a canonical row unless opted in) and giving all 16 IFAF men's teams distinct `-M`-suffixed codes in `team_mapping.csv` for when a user does opt in. Final corpus: exactly 32 women's games / 3,191 rows from IFAF, 0 men's rows, 28,255 total rows across all five sources — back to the pre-session baseline, now with real yardage/yards-to-go/EP/WP for the women's rows.

## Decisions Made

- `yards_to_go` IS derivable from field position alone (IFAF's own four-downs-to-midfield/four-downs-to-goal rule) — reversing the initial-round finding that it was structurally unavailable. The initial finding was correct about the API's own `yardsToGo` field (still a hardcoded `10` everywhere), but wrong to conclude the *value* couldn't be derived from other already-available fields.
- Between a "sticky" (drive-persistent) and a "non-sticky" (per-play recompute) crossing rule, chose non-sticky based on a direct empirical comparison against the events feed's own `MIDDLE`/`GOAL` marker (98.2% vs. 74.3% agreement) — a live-data finding that IFAF's own engine does not persist a first-down-style achievement across a drive.
- The 2026-08-17/2026-09-06 corpus regression is fixed per-game by ingesting both candidate snapshots through the real `validation.checks.run_checks` suite and keeping whichever one the check suite accepts, rather than a blanket revert (which would have discarded 31 women's games' worth of otherwise-fine 2026-09-06 data and had no fallback at all for the men's tournament).
- `all_games`/`include_full` added as opt-in parameters defaulting to `False` so every existing test and caller of `fetch_tournament` is unaffected — new behavior is purely additive.
- `redact_pii` placed as a single choke point inside `_write_json` rather than at each call site, so no future endpoint addition can accidentally skip redaction.
- Video-marks table kept as a standalone artifact outside the canonical schema (it has no equivalent in `CANONICAL_COLUMNS` and isn't meant to feed the model — it's a film-review aid).
- Competition disambiguated by `tournament.divisions[0]`, not `tournament.name` — both IFAF tournaments share the identical name, which had been silently merging the men's tournament into the women's competition identity.
- The new `mens-international` competition tier is deliberately NOT added to `model/hyperparams.py`'s frozen `TIER_FEATURE_COLUMNS` (the champion models' already-trained feature list) — no retraining implied. Training exclusion instead uses the existing per-game-id `exclude_games_ep`/`exclude_games_wp` mechanism, already proven for two legacy games.
- The M3 explosiveness/comparison corpus scope fix lives in `scripts/explosiveness_comparison.py` (a standalone script), not in `features/explosiveness.py::scrimmage_plays` — `scrimmage_plays` has no source/team scoping at all and is shared by every caller across the codebase; the exclusion belongs at the corpus-loading boundary instead, mirroring where `reports/own_team.py` already scopes by team.

- **Round 2's training-only exclusion was insufficient and got superseded.** Excluding men's game ids from `exclude_games_ep`/`exclude_games_wp` kept them out of training but not out of `plays.parquet`/`plays_scored.parquet` themselves, and `m-ger`/`w-ger` colliding onto canonical team code `GER` meant every OTHER team-scoped consumer (own-team/opponent reports, HC splits, any ad-hoc `scrimmage_plays` caller besides the one M3 script that got an explicit tier filter) would still have mixed the two national teams. Fixed at the actual root: `sources.ifaf.ingest_tournaments` now gates `ingest_snapshots` itself, so a tournament not opted into never becomes a canonical row at all — the corpus is safe by construction, not by an ever-growing list of downstream exclusions. `exclude_games_ep`/`exclude_games_wp` reverted to just the two original legacy exclusions.
- Distinct `-M`-suffixed canonical team codes (`team_mapping.csv`) for all 16 IFAF men's teams, so the opt-in case (a user adding `"ffwc26-men"` to `ingest_tournaments`) can never silently merge the men's national team with the women's under one team identity.

## Nachtrag 2026-09-07 (second follow-up, same day) — no-play down exemption + fallback exclusion

A coordinator audit of the fix above found two further gaps, both fixed the same day.

**1. `downs_range` now exempts no-play penalty rows from the null-`down` check.** 17 of the 29 `/plays`-primary games were quarantined solely by 46 null-`down` rows; 38 of those are penalty-only `no_play` records (a dead-ball foul never reaches a snap and has no down of its own by definition) — a classification gap in the check, not a real data gap. `downs_range` (`flag_football_ep.validation.checks`) now tolerates a null `down` exactly on `play_type == "no_play"` AND `penalty == 1`; every other null `down` still fails exactly as before. Fixing this also surfaced a real bug: `flatten_plays_records` was zeroing `penalty` on every nullified record (grouped with the other scoring/turnover flags), so a nullified penalty-only record lost the very flag the new exemption keys on — `penalty` is now set unconditionally (a record-shape classification, not an effect), while every other flag stays suppressed on nullified rows as before. **The QF game's own quarantine was exactly this one row** (`play_id 6`, a dead-ball penalty) — it is now fully accepted (93 rows) in `plays.parquet` itself, not just visible via the CSV export. 12 games total are saved by this fix; 5 remain quarantined on genuine charting gaps (never fabricated).

**2. The 13 `unified-plays`-fallback games are now excluded, not accepted.** They were being accepted on `unified-plays.context` — the exact source the first fix had already proven unreliable, the inverse of "safe by construction." An events-feed reconstruction of pre-snap state (replay `POSSESSION_CHANGE`/`DOWN_UPDATE`/`LOS_UPDATE`/`TRY_DOWN`/`STATUS_CHANGE`/`MANUAL_EDIT` in `sequenceNumber` order, using each event's own top-level `down`/`ballOn`/`half` snapshot fields) was built and measured against the 29 games with real `/plays` to check against: **77.5% down agreement, 46.8% ballOn agreement** — both well under the 95% bar required before a reconstructed source may feed the corpus. ## Nachtrag 2026-09-07 (tenth follow-up, same day) — a third `ballOn` reconstruction attempt, structurally aligned, also measured and declined

The owner re-raised the QF's own null-`ballOn` gap directly (from play 24 on, every position-related field is empty because 71 of 93 `/plays` records have `ballOn = null`). A third, methodically different attempt at the third follow-up's own open problem: replay `events_{id}.json` as an explicit pre-snap state machine (`ingest.ifaf.replay_events_los_states`), then align the emitted state sequence to `/plays`' own real records *structurally* — team and down, within each `POSSESSION_CHANGE`-bounded possession, bounded `lookahead=1`, never crossing a possession boundary (`_extract_real_down_records`/`_segment_records_by_team`/`align_events_los_states`).

**Validated on 21 zero-null-`ballOn` women's games** (more than the 16 originally scoped — the extra 5 are games quarantined for unrelated reasons that don't affect `ballOn` completeness, strictly more validation power): **16.8% exact agreement (292/1,735), down agreement 41.9%, `|Δ| ≤ 2` agreement 18.9%** — worse than all three previously-measured reconstructions (58.8%, 31.4%, 46.8%), not an improvement. Root cause, found live: a real down with no yardage change (an incomplete pass, a sack at the line) fires no `LOS_UPDATE` of its own at all, so the *next* `LOS_UPDATE` gets misattributed to the wrong down, and the misalignment cascades for the rest of that possession. A hypothesized `ballOn` scale flip (some games' `TRY_DOWN` events carry the mirrored `50 - x` value) was checked directly and does not explain the gap — an oracle upper bound (best-of flip/no-flip per game) scores identically, 16.8%.

**Decision: not adopted, again.** No `ballOn` fabricated. `replay_events_los_states`/`align_events_los_states`/`validate_events_los_fill`/`diagnose_partial_los_fill` (`ingest/ifaf.py`) are kept as tested, permanently-available diagnostic tooling (`_LOS_FILL_GATE_THRESHOLD = 0.95`), never wired into `flatten_plays_records`/`ingest_snapshots`. `diagnose_partial_los_fill` gives each of the 8 partially-spotted games a precise diagnostic (null-`ballOn` count, `LOS_UPDATE` events available, structural-match candidates that are NOT more likely to be correct than the 16.8% baseline) instead of a further guess. `ep`/`epa`/`wp`/`wpa` coverage unchanged. 21 new synthetic-fixture tests added to `tests/test_ingest_ifaf.py`; full suite passes.
