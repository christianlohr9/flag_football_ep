## Accomplishments

- `read_legacy`/`get_games`/`legacy_mutations`/`ingest_legacy` implement the full `<interfaces>` contract: `data_raw.csv` (47 games, 3,701 plays) ingests into `CANONICAL_COLUMNS` with `game_id` rewritten to `legacy-{n}` and the original integer preserved as `source_game_id`.
- `get_games` reproduces the notebook's "first drive is home" assumption without `.melt`/`.pivot(aggregate_function=...)`, verified against RESEARCH Pitfall 5's stated risk; a game with three distinct `posteam` values raises `ValueError` naming the game instead of silently producing a malformed struct.
