Put a human between the generated German proposal and the head coach.

coach that his workbook formula and his spoken description of it disagree, and in which words. That
is a relationship judgement, and the numbers behind it were derived by reverse-engineering his
spreadsheet. The user reads both documents once, corrects the tone or the framing, and only then do
they become the October-sync handout.

Purpose: this is the only artifact of the phase another person reads.
